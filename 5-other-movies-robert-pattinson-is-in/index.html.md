# 5 Other Movies Robert Pattinson Is In \(Beyond Twilight\)

Personally, I think Robert Pattinson is a remarkably talented actor. At every chance I get, I watch a movie that he plays in just to admire his incredible acting range. If you are searching for movies Robert Pattinson is in beyond his most famous blockbuster roles, there is a fascinating list of early films with Robert Pattinson that show off his versatility. Before he became a global household name, Pattinson took on diverse characters in everything from psychological BBC dramas like [The Haunted Airman](#5) to emotional feature films like [Remember Me](#1). Below, I am going to share 5 other movies Robert Pattinson starred in that every fan should check out.

Here are a few quick highlights of why exploring these early Robert Pattinson films is so rewarding:

- Showcases his remarkable acting range across different genres before mega-stardom
- Features compelling performances in both indie films and major studio productions
- Includes standout roles alongside acclaimed international actors and directors

## 5 The Haunted Airman 2006

In the 2006 psychological thriller film [The Haunted Airman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunted_Airman), Robert Pattinson played the lead role of Toby Jugg, a paralyzed RAF pilot recovering in a mysterious remote hospital. I have to admit, I originally watched this movie just because it features Robert Pattinson, but his intense performance as a troubled airman navigating paranoia and psychological tension really stands out. For anyone looking for early films with Robert Pattinson from his [2006 acting era](#5), this atmospheric drama is a fascinating watch.

## 4 The Bad Mother's Handbook 2007

In the 2007 British television drama [The Bad Mother's Handbook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bad_Mother%27s_Handbook), Robert played the lovable nerd Daniel Gale. Have any of you watched this one? It is a charming story about three generations of women, and Pattinson gives a delightfully charming performance. If you haven't watched it yet and want to learn more about the early movies Robert Pattinson played in, it is definitely a hidden gem worth seeking out.

## 3 Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix 2007

Oh yeah, we all know that he appeared in [Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter_and_the_Order_of_the_Phoenix_(film)) in 2007. He played the heroic Cedric Diggory in the franchise (introduced primarily in the fourth installment), appearing in flashback and archive footage in [Order of the Phoenix](#3). Don’t you think he made a cute Cedric? The funny thing is, up until Twilight came out, I did not know much about Robert Pattinson, so I didn’t even realize he was the same actor in Harry Potter, even though I watched the films many times—Cedric never caught my eye back then!

## 2 Little Ashes 2009

In the 2009 biographical drama [Little Ashes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Ashes), Robert Pattinson took on the challenging role of the surrealist painter Salvador Dalí. How many of you have watched this movie? His portrayal of Dalí's youth, eccentric artist persona, and complex relationships in 1920s Madrid showed how serious he was about choosing bold, unconventional roles in movies by Robert Pattinson.

## 1 Remember Me 2010

In [Remember Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remember_Me_(2010_film)) (2010), he starred as Tyler Hawkins, a rebellious young man in New York struggling with family tragedy and romance. I must say that I waited for this movie to come out for a long time. It isn’t just because the actor from Twilight is in it, but because I knew it would be a captivating drama. I really liked how the story sounded, and the book adaptation elements were equally powerful. [Remember Me](#1) was definitely a great movie that showcased his ability as a leading man.

Some of the other notable movies Robert Pattinson starred in around that time include _Bel Ami_ and _Water for Elephants_. Looking back across his entire filmography, he has consistently proven to be a deeply talented and daring actor.
