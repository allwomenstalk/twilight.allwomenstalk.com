# Why Underworld: Evolution Still Reigns in the Vampire vs Werewolf Genre: 5 Reasons to Rewatch

_Underworld: Evolution_ arrived as the action-packed 2006 sequel to the original 2003 hit _Underworld_, bringing back [Kate Beckinsale](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000295/) in her iconic role as the vampire Death Dealer, Selene. When evaluating [underworld vs twilight](https://twilight.allwomenstalk.com/), this franchise takes a noticeably darker, more visceral approach to supernatural lore. I really liked this movie upon its release and still think it managed to outshine the first installment in sheer scale and mythology. Below, I am going to give you 5 reasons I liked _Underworld: Evolution_ and why it remains a standout favorite in gothic cinema.

- Intense, choreography-driven combat sequences
- Deeper expansion of ancient vampire and werewolf origin stories
- High-stakes character choices and lore shifts

## 5. Vampires Vs. Werewolves

I liked the fact that it was showing us a full-scale ancestral war featuring Lycans against vampires. Every single time a vampire encountered a werewolf on screen, it was an absolute must-see clash of strength and speed. The tension between the species reached a whole new level here, which ties directly into how Selene navigates the threat of the ancient [vampire Elders](#3).

## 4. Werewolves Could Control Themselves

I never thought this level of control was possible in classic monster lore. I originally assumed that whenever a werewolf got upset or transformed, they automatically turned into a mindless beast with zero restraint. However, in _Underworld_, they showed us that certain humans could actually retain control over when and how they turned into a wolf. The evolution of the [underworld evolution werewolf](https://www.sonypictures.com/movies/underworldevolution) mechanics gave the Lycans strategic depth rather than just making them random monsters.

## 3. Awakening Marcus

When two out of the 3 vampire Elders are destroyed in the previous conflict, Selene hopes to awaken the last remaining Elder, Marcus. Marcus is still in hibernation and holds the key to revealing the true historical past before Kraven can assassinate him. Unraveling the mythology of the [underworld 3 elders](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0401855/) added a rich historical layer to the plot, showing that the history of the [underworld vampire elders](https://twilight.allwomenstalk.com/) was far more complex than originally presented. I found this plot point to be one of the most compelling parts of the whole story.

- Marcus Corvinus: The original vampire ancestor
- William Corvinus: The original werewolf ancestor
- Alexander Corvinus: The immortal father who holds the true hybrid key

## 2. Selene Drinks Blood

In order to pass on the legendary hybrid legacy and survive the coming storm, Selene drinks Alexander’s ancestral blood. Gaining immense power from the progenitor himself transformed her capabilities, right before Alexander ends up blowing himself up—which was a real cool and dramatic moment in the film. This momentous decision directly sets up the high-stakes confrontation during [the ending](#1).

## 1. The Ending

Oh yeah, I definitely loved the ending of _Underworld_, and the thrilling payoff in the final act is definitely a primary reason to watch it. Revisiting the iconic [underworld evolution ending](https://twilight.allwomenstalk.com/) reveals just how satisfyingly it wrapped up the core conflict while redefining Selene's place in the supernatural world.
